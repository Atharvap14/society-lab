# What the lab measures now

Implementation key, 4 October 2026. This distinguishes available instruments from proposed research definitions. It is not a behavior ontology, a calibration claim or a new scientific result. The [communication framework](communication-theory.md) supplies questions; an implemented field supplies only its stated measurement.

## Current measurements

| Instrument | Implemented readout | What remains unknown |
|---|---|---|
| [Message screening](../../swarm_lab/discovery.py) | Eight named regex labels: commitment, completion report, evidence request, blocker report, correction, human help request, acknowledgement and plan change. Context/lexical motifs nominate incidents. | A hit is text screening, not an accepted obligation, verified completion, successful repair or mental state. Negation, quotation, scope and missing context can defeat interpretation. Optional typed Laya/Jev measurements retain abstention/failure; the present corpus has no established semantic calibration. |
| [Measurement review](../../swarm_lab/measurement_adjudication.py) | A frozen binary construct, seeded sample from an exact retained dataset, declared yes/no/uncertain judgments and revision history. Optional prediction agreement uses known labels with available predictions. | Reviewer identity/mode is declared; latest judgment is a reference, not authenticated human truth. Missing/uncertain cases are excluded from comparison, not negative examples. Message sampling does not estimate episode prevalence or establish population accuracy. |
| [Graphs](../../swarm_lab/network.py) and [window leads](../../swarm_lab/graph_discovery.py) | Separately typed reference/reply and inferred proximity/lexical projections; counts, centrality, reciprocity, static reachability and selected contrasts. Name sensitivity and fixed-population audits expose extraction choices. | Naming is not delivery; adjacency is not exposure. Roles, tasks, inactive referenced targets, short-name eligibility, quotations and data-selected windows remain alternatives. A deleted graph node is not an agent-removal intervention. |
| [Node spectra](../../swarm_lab/spectral_observables.py) and [edge Hodge algebra](../../swarm_lab/graph_hodge_workflow.py) | Regex-hit proportions with authored-message denominators; grouped energy on a disclosed symmetrized normalized Laplacian. The source-bound Hodge workflow decomposes net directional reference counts into gradient/circulation. | Referenced nonauthors have unknown rates. An induced-author graph changes the population/operator. Modes are not latent traits; circulation is not rumor or physical traffic. Reverse counts cancel; chronology is discarded. Source-bound Hodge uses unspecified faces, so curl/harmonic are unknown. No manifold/LBO approximation is established. |
| [Temporal references](../../swarm_lab/temporal_network.py) and [timing reference](../../swarm_lab/temporal_null_models.py) | Strict timestamp-increasing, same-room reference paths and deletion losses; bounded message-group timestamp permutations on frozen selected windows. | Paths retain reference direction, not observed information flow. Equal-time edges cannot chain. Descriptive counts/envelopes supply no p-value, confidence interval, independent replication or causal effect. Interpreting them as inferential null comparisons would require justified exchangeability. |
| [Indexed emissions](../../swarm_lab/indexed_event_audit.py), [actor/time](../../swarm_lab/actor_event_queries.py), [literal markers](../../swarm_lab/wait_marker_alignment.py) | Typed exported-field matches, exact actor/time action queries and literal-marker proximity candidates with bounded scope. | Emission is not receipt/consumption. Missing event rooms stay missing; START/STOP do not establish leases. Marker proximity is not semantic detector accuracy or inactivity; clock relationships, censoring and event reuse remain explicit. |
| [Outcome and claim checks](../../swarm_lab/claim_audit.py) | Registered code-oracle outcomes, finite typed report facts, scoped inspection distinctions and independently replayed actions/archives. | Checked arithmetic or replay does not verify free prose, latent reasoning, historical mechanism fit, provider consumption or generalization. Incomplete executions provide no subset effect estimate. |

## Research definitions, not automatic metrics

The provisional **grounding ledger** in the framework is not a populated historical common-ground database. Presentation, receipt, interpretation, agreement and execution need separately identified evidence; politeness cannot fill them.

**Commitment reliability/age and ownership ambiguity** require adjudicated obligations, acceptance where relevant, cancellation/reassignment, evaluable deadlines and verified discharge. Current commitment/completion regex motifs do not compute these measures. **Repair success/latency and correction persistence** require a frozen trouble/resolution criterion, referent tracking, relevant opportunity and censoring; a correction label or later successful oracle answer is insufficient.

**Evidence independence, adoption, memory transformation, norms and useful contribution** need further source lineage, access/use, comparable memory or task-utility evidence. Synthetic generators can declare original sources and score answers, but those world facts do not establish historical knowledge, trust, culture or leadership. Novelty remains unestablished without appropriate comparison and literature search. These gaps may motivate a narrowly defined binary review or a new reviewed world; they should not be silently replaced with an available proxy.

## Six study interfaces, four compiler families, five worlds

| Study interface | World / compiler selection | Code-checked primary outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Artifact handoff | `shared_artifact_coordination` | Correct published manifest |
| Noisy source diffusion | `provenance_diffusion` | Mean correct binary verdicts |
| Complementary information | `complementary_information` | Mean exact modular-total submissions |
| Shared resource | `exclusive_resource_tasks` | Executed eight-task completion fraction |
| Triggered resource reminders | Same resource world; separate paired timing protocol | Paired whole-swarm completion difference |
| Correction relay | Separate revision-capable world and four-cell protocol | Exact final C-answer accuracy and assigned-policy block contrast |

The [compiler catalog](../../swarm_lab/environment_authoring.py) and [registration bridge](../../swarm_lab/experiment_authoring.py) support the first four families. [Timed resource](../../swarm_lab/timed_resource_experiments.py) reuses the resource environment under its own timing contract; [relay](../../swarm_lab/revision_relay_experiments.py) adds the fifth distinct world. Neither is automatically authored or dispatched by the generic four-family cycle. The [choice map](experiment-choice-map.md) specifies assignments, falsifiers and fit limits. A world-fit approval is not evidence that its fixed treatment/outcome isolates the proposed mechanism.

Keep source observation, annotated pattern, mechanism hypothesis and experimental result separate in the [library](../../swarm_lab/library.py). `infrastructure_tested`, `pilot_tested` and `replication_tested` record scoped execution/evidence history; they do not establish a universal mechanism. Initial theories remain hypotheses; quantitative directional evidence and rival/null results stay separate. Scripts test feasibility and mechanics, while model effects require explicit live registration and adequate inference. Local Study-guide motivation is not a registered protocol source, and no graph or review result automatically promotes a theory.
