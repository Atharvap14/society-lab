## Four explanations worth separating

**Artifact dependency versus narration.** The narrow historical hypothesis is that an unavailable shared object gates particular downstream work. A rival is coordinated status narration: agents repeat ownership or completion language while work proceeds independently. L4 remains a hypothesis; L2's infrastructure-tested label describes scripts. The April 22 reports support a dependency *account*, not an observed state transition. The source chronology also rejects treating the later status question as causing the earlier blank-chart report. Exact source pins are in the [selected-lead interpretation](selected-lead-interpretation.md).

The smallest historical discriminator is independently linked artifact-version transitions and downstream actions, including resumptions before the claimed release. That requires additional tool/artifact evidence. The artifact world supports private reminder/neutral/baseline assignment. Separately randomizing completion-claim strength or source reliability while fixing object state requires a new reviewed protocol. Failure to change correct publication would challenge that prediction, but not erase textual handoffs. E2 supports reminder versus neutral in this task; the strong baseline and unrandomized inspection/repair paths leave neutral-note disruption and broader task mobilization unresolved.

**Routing bottleneck versus local inference.** E3 allows high accuracy with minimal exchange; E4 produces exchange without correct integration. Neither licenses “more connectivity helps” or “agents cannot cooperate.” S1/S2 show that apparent bridge dependence can reflect short-name exclusion, denominators and backwards chronology. Algebraic reachability is not evidence that an intermediary transmitted a needed fact.

The new revision-relay capability is the most economical prospective discriminator: assign whole fresh teams to early/late correction availability at B's scheduled first decision, crossed with informative/sham direct delivery to C. Pair source values, private residue and action-independent schedules within seed blocks; retain identical opportunities and sham contacts. Primary outcome is exact C final revision/modular answer. The block interaction is `(late-sham − early-sham) − (late-informative − early-informative)`. A sufficiently precise absence of the predicted rescue would challenge the timing-policy account. All cells already admit successful scripts, falsifying structural impossibility. This is assigned availability, not receipt-conditioned mediation: bypass also changes attribution, salience and draft context. A private-thought efficacy question needs a separate factor/design.

**Task-specific waiting versus global blockage.** L2 describes particular artifact dependencies; a stronger story says an agent treats unavailable computer access as blocking all work. S4's scoped zero and S5's missing-room actions demonstrate an observation-predicate problem, not a behavioral contradiction. Literal “wait” can be quoted, negated or prospective; nearby WAIT is not ground-truth inactivity. No room may be inferred from actor/time coincidence.

The timed-resource world can compare reminder/neutral content under one fixed `executed_wait_independent_pending` trigger, with paired exogenous paths and whole-swarm completion as the outcome, including never-delivered units. Independent task execution while the computer is closed is a concrete falsifier of universal dependency *in that analogue*. Historical exclusivity, queues, multiple computers and real browser dependencies are unsupported needs; current resource rules invent them. Comparing trigger policies requires a new design, not conditioning on realized waits.

**Version coordination versus latent verification.** Correct publication or correction use is executed behavior; it does not establish trust, belief revision or semantic inspection. E2's equal any-agent current-version inspection rates directly block the claim that the observed arm difference simply demonstrates more such inspections. Correct outcomes without that metric are possible. Revision relay supplies explicit draft/retain/revise opportunities, but E5 is scripted. A correct final guess scores without a receipt gate; an invalid final answer scores zero. No behavioral effect has yet been measured there.

Prospectively, freeze answer accuracy separately from forwarding, canonical attachment, constructed request and revision fidelity. Do not adjust the primary contrast for these post-treatment measurements. Selective route removal, matched delivery quotas, uncertain provenance or mediator interventions require additional reviewed designs. Existing capabilities and gaps are mapped in [experiment choice](experiment-choice-map.md); none supplies private latent-state editing or verified provider consumption.
