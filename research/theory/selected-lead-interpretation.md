Those reports support a narrow textual episode hypothesis. They do not verify chart state, actual inactivity, reception, or causal unblocking. Moreover, this candidate's narrative comparison_ids span April 16–22 and include human context. They are a broader interpretive source set, not the formal April 22 19h graph comparator. Preserve that distinction.

The audit rejects a strong claim that the old bridge magnitude demonstrates unique behavioral mediation. It does not falsify the narrower explicit wait/update pattern, and does not establish its recurrence or causal mechanism.

**Incoming-owner candidate.** Its seed is graph-lead-c7af8e6e02732836, the April 18 19h→April 17 19h contrast, not the reversed April 22 lead. Its own native incoming difference drops from +0.637318 to +0.231094 under short expansion; its fixed-pair baseline is +0.597485 and expanded difference +0.270704. The combined instrument has the same values as short expansion for that pair. The sign therefore survives in its own audited windows.

The April 22 reversal cannot directly falsify that candidate's source contrast. It does show why a general ownership interpretation cannot treat all roster-name matches as incoming requests to the owner. To test its mechanism, identify ownership independently from tool/artifact records and distinguish requests to the owner from references about the owner. A surviving name-concentration contrast alone does not establish that people route work because of ownership.

## Discriminating checks and held-out hypotheses

1. **Adjudicate speech acts before interpreting contacts.** Blindly label all 114 retained messages in the two windows, including the 99 agent-authored messages, rather than only name hits. Separate direct address/request, status report, artifact attribution, endorsement/quotation, model reference, and ambiguity. Allow multi-label messages; do not equate the first regex span with message-level intent. Measure agreement and unresolved cases on a separately sampled label set before claiming calibrated address precision.
2. **Check repetition and phase.** Keep the one-message/target instrument intact while separately reporting a predeclared deduplicated-status diagnostic. Identify episode boundaries without optimizing the graph difference. Repeated process feedback, chart publication, and later campaign-scope discussion are different task phases; adjacency alone does not make them causal controls.
3. **Verify claimed state and temporal edges.** Link lock/unlock, publication, and subsequent inspection to exact artifact versions/tool records if available. Require a putative response to follow its request; examine out-of-room routes and preexisting information. Screenshots or tool traces may establish an artifact action but still need a receipt/inspection rule to establish transfer.
4. **Freeze a held-out owner prediction.** In new episodes, independently coded owner changes should predict which agent receives adjudicated work requests, after declared activity/task-phase adjustment. A stable recipient across owner changes favors a standing reporting role; a shift only in artifact attributions favors narrative concentration. Predeclare exposure proxies and keep unknown readers unknown.
5. **Freeze a held-out dependency prediction.** Explicit artifact dependencies should predict subsequent verified work resumption after a verified artifact transition, relative to matched episodes without that dependency. Continued waiting after an available artifact, resumption before it, or waiting for unrelated computer access are discriminating outcomes. Treat silent intervals and right-censoring as unknown rather than inactivity.
6. **Use formatting as a measurement control.** Re-encode identical held-out transcripts with declared short/full names and dash variants, then compare extraction. This tests instrument sensitivity on fixed content, not changes in agent behavior. A future agent experiment must separately manipulate substantive role/visibility/context under an appropriate protocol and log delivery; renaming text is not evidence for that mechanism.

These are proposed checks, not completed validation or funded experiments. Selection across features/windows, reused evidence, unobserved exposure, ordinary resource etiquette, and heterogeneous task phases remain alternatives. No novelty, causal network effect, historical mediation, or theory promotion follows from this note.
