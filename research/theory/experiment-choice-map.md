# From a behavioral anomaly to an executable experiment

Method selection note, 4 October 2026. This maps the current interfaces; it reports no new experiment or behavioral finding. **Supported** means an executable, scoped design exists. It does not mean the design reconstructs the Village or isolates every proposed mechanism. Start with the outcome and a falsifiable alternative, then choose the smallest world that can measure both.

## Choose the question before the template

| Question suggested by an anomaly | Supported assignment and treatment package | Estimand and executable readout | Main limit of the interpretation |
|---|---|---|---|
| A confident completion claim precedes publication: would an evidence-check reminder improve the published object? | [Artifact protocol](../../swarm_lab/experiments.py), `create_protocol`: fresh three-agent teams, baseline/placebo/evidence-thought arms; private insertion to the coordinator before its first decision. Inspection, builder repair, messaging and coordinator publication are legal. | Whole-team ITT risk difference, evidence-thought minus placebo, in code-checked successful publication. Incorrect publication or no publication within budget scores zero. First publication terminates the run. | Correctness of the executed manifest is measured; private verification, trust, independent task completion and later recovery are not. Note content, extra context and within-team spillover form a package. |
| Repeated reports look like independent evidence: does provenance guidance change binary decisions? | [Diffusion protocol](../../swarm_lab/diffusion_experiments.py), `create_diffusion_protocol`: whole four-agent networks; named ring/star/complete topologies crossed with context. Four reports contain three independent noisy measurements, including a shared-source duplicate. Default recipient is agent-0. | Context effect averaged over registered topologies, and a registered topology contrast when applicable; network mean of four correct binary verdicts, missing verdicts zero. Original-source inventory and forwarding lineage are secondary descriptions. | Copy correlation is fixed, not a randomized duplication factor. Topology changes degree, routes and opportunity; a source reminder effect does not isolate echo amplification, independent sensor weighting or leadership. |
| A team needs several distinct contributions: does communication opportunity help combine them? | [Complementary protocol](../../swarm_lab/complementary_experiments.py), `create_complementary_protocol`: independent four-agent networks; topology × placebo/provenance reminder, fixed recipient agent-0. Own residue is initially visible; canonical bundles can be sent to neighbors. | Factor ITT contrasts on mean exact submitted modular-total accuracy, missing submissions zero. Each of four uniform residues contributes to the oracle. Canonical coverage at submission and recipient deliveries describe the process. | Missing any residue leaves the target uniform under this generator, but missing an attachment does not prove missing knowledge: free text can convey values. Multicast fanout is unmatched across graphs. Agreement is not the primary outcome. |
| Agents wait while another task remains legal: does a task-specific reminder change executed work? | [Resource protocol](../../swarm_lab/resource_experiments.py), `create_resource_protocol`: fresh four-agent swarms, reminder/neutral note before agent-0's first decision. Each agent has independent work and computer-gated work; an external release and exclusive lease govern the latter. | Whole-swarm ITT difference in eight-task executed completion fraction. Independent/computer task completion, waits, grants and denials are descriptive readouts. | Global computer exclusivity, task costs and release schedule are invented analogue facts. Waiting does not identify a global-wait belief, and denials do not all mean contention. Independently seeded arms match distributions, not realized order/release. |
| The same reminder might be more relevant at a visible trouble boundary: does an observed-state policy help? | [Timed resource protocol](../../swarm_lab/timed_resource_experiments.py), `create_timed_resource_protocol`: one active and one neutral swarm per seed block, identical precomputed exogenous paths. One fixed-recipient insertion under `first_decision`, `executed_wait_independent_pending`, or `first_observed_resource_open`. | Mean paired whole-swarm completion difference, including completed never-delivered units. Seed blocks supply uncertainty units. Trigger evaluations, eligibility status, exact local request receipts and applied actions are separate measurements. | This compares content under one registered trigger, not different trigger timings. Receipt is request construction, not provider consumption. Conditioning on receipt or waits would change the estimand; unresolved eligibility remains unknown. |
| A correction arrives around a relay decision: does an alternate route rescue downstream accuracy? | [Revision-relay protocol](../../swarm_lab/revision_relay_experiments.py), `create_revision_relay_protocol`: four fresh two-subject teams per block; early/late correction to B × informative/sham direct bypass to C. Scripted A, fixed B/C/B/C opportunities, paired exogenous records and target. | Exact C final-answer accuracy per cell; block contrast **D = (late-sham − early-sham) − (late-informative − early-informative)**. A correct guess scores even without a canonical correction attachment. | Assigned timing/route interaction is not mediated relay dependence. Chronology, salience, source attribution and repetition remain package differences. Current runner inserts no private thought and supplies no interaction p-value. |

All these units allow interference inside a team. Agents, messages, tasks and turns are measurements within a unit, not additional randomized replicates. Assignment design, exposure mapping and estimand must be stated separately before making agent-level direct or spillover claims. [Aronow and Samii, pinned v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.6156v4).

## Cheapest falsifier first; extensions remain explicit

| Candidate story | Zero-model check or counterexample now | Extension requiring a new reviewed design |
|---|---|---|
| Unchecked claim adoption causes artifact failure | Replay an actual publication against requirements; script a reasonable inspection/repair route. A public assertion alone cannot establish object failure. | Randomize claim strength or source reliability while holding object state fixed; add post-publication revision if recovery is the question. |
| Duplicates drive overconfidence | Verify which reports actually share an original source, and contrast scripts that count originals versus report IDs. | Randomize copy multiplicity with the same original measurements; introduce uncertain/forged provenance. Current source IDs are trustworthy world facts. |
| A nominated relay is indispensable | Exhibit legal all-gather paths; in the relay world, test late/no-bypass solvability. All four relay cells admit successful scripts. | Selective edge removal, matched delivery quotas, delays or randomized recipient position. A ring/complete contrast alone cannot isolate centrality. |
| Resource blockage causes universal inactivity | Check explicit task dependencies and source reports of preparation or simultaneous sessions; script legal independent work during closure. | Multiple resources, reoccupation, queues, uncertain availability or real browser tasks. Historical exclusivity must not be supplied by missing chats. |
| A trouble-triggered thought necessarily helps | Replay public own-history boundaries; include never-eligible, unknown and failed-delivery fixtures. A note-sensitive fixture tests insertion plumbing only. | Randomize trigger policies, or repeated eligible decisions with a new sequential estimator. The standalone communication-gap trigger is not an integrated timed communication experiment. |
| B's realized receipt explains the timing effect | Compare assigned relay cells without filtering on actual forwarding or C receipt. Check chronology and sham slots in the archive. | Additional recipient/reminder factors or a defensible mediation design. No-receipt subgroups and all-label permutations do not identify an interaction with unrestricted main effects. |

Before selecting a world, distinguish a name reference, recorded emission, scoped queue delivery, constructed request, attempted tool action, artifact transition and task outcome. [Source links](../../swarm_lab/source_links.py) and [indexed event matching](../../swarm_lab/indexed_event_audit.py) preserve explicit keys and unknown/conflicting fields; they do not establish historical consumption. Partial source coverage cannot establish global absence. Existing [communication falsifiers](communication-falsifiers.md) constrain the resource story before any reminder test.

## Registration and reading the result

Freeze exact sources, capabilities, backend, notes, recipient, action costs, horizon, allocation, outcome, primary family and inference settings. Independent draws in the artifact/diffusion/complementary/resource designs must not be retrospectively presented as pairs. Timed-resource pairs bind the entire initial world and exogenous schedule; relay pairs bind truth and scheduled opportunities while intentionally changing deliveries. Equal paths do not equalize chosen actions, stopping or realized access.

Tiny pilots have hard limits. Two independent runs per resource arm cannot attain a two-sided exact p below 1/3; two timed seed blocks cannot go below 1/2. The default eight-network complementary design has a 2/36 factor-test floor, so Holm cannot reject at 0.05. Relay p-values are unavailable; its interval is also unavailable unless the registered independence/stability assumptions are explicitly declared, and a small-block bound remains wide. Null results, ceilings and collapsed bootstrap sensitivities do not establish equivalence.

Unknown or invalid behavioral actions consume their declared opportunities and remain in ITT under each runner's contract. Infrastructure-incomplete grids suppress outcome estimation rather than selecting successful runs. Independent replay can check archived states, requests, actions and numerical facts; it does not authenticate provider consumption or semantic adoption. Scripts establish feasibility and integration, including negative cases; their arm differences are scripted-policy behavior, not model effects.

Achieved centrality, information coverage, waits and receipt are post-treatment process descriptions. Adjusting for them can select different networks or remove a treatment pathway. Mechanism tests need additional manipulable factors and assumptions, not a more confident label on the same contrast.

Capability-driven [authoring](../../swarm_lab/environment_authoring.py) can select the four existing template families or return unsupported; compilation is not historical fidelity. Timing and correction relay have separate reviewed contracts. Persistent society memory, arbitrary populations, strategic incentives, browser execution and latent-state editing remain gaps. InterWhen motivates extraction/verification/feedback boundaries; this lab's observable context insertion does not implement its token rollback. [InterWhen v3, §§4.1–4.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11202v3#S4.SS1). Repeated-decision randomization would require availability and history assumptions beyond current whole-team inference. [Boruvka et al., v3](https://arxiv.org/html/1601.00237v3#S4).
