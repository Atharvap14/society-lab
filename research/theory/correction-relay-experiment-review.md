# Independent review of the prospective correction-relay design

Review date: October 4, 2026. **The narrowed design is a plausible prospective timing-policy probe, with no remaining identification defect found within its stated scope.** This judgment does not approve an implemented environment, register a protocol, estimate an effect or identify a historical communication mechanism. No source, subject or provider was run; no production record or frozen code was changed.

The reviewed [draft](correction-relay-experiment-draft.md) snapshot had SHA-256 `bb8f3b797fd5e7ee65026e26ca7e489ec2ee189cd52bd8067d06512304740bbe`. It uses scripted source A, model subjects B/C, two decisions per subject and an assigned early/late correction crossed with an assigned informative/noninformative bypass. The following adapter and registration requirements are part of this review boundary, rather than evidence that the system already implements them.

## What the randomized contrast can answer

Freeze the primary interaction as

`D = (late − early without informative bypass) − (late − early with informative bypass)`.

For final binary correctness, each block contrast lies in `[-2, 2]`. A negative value agrees with the proposed directional prediction: the late disadvantage is smaller under assigned informative bypass. It does not identify relay receipt as a mediator or prove that the disadvantage was caused specifically by B failing to communicate. Direct source authority, repetition, salience, provisional-answer anchoring and context order can change the same interaction.

Each whole team is an assigned unit, with intended B→C interference within the team. A seed block groups four independently reset teams with shared exogenous truth/schedule, one per cell. Randomized cell-to-execution-slot assignment, fresh subject sessions and absence of cross-team state are necessary execution contracts. Shared world seeds do not synchronize model sampling. Use blocks for uncertainty about the block-average interaction; the four cells and their individual decisions are not four independent replications of that interaction.

The draft correctly distinguishes an all-cell sharp global no-policy-effect permutation null from zero interaction when main effects may exist. Two blocks are useful for mechanics and a weak descriptive estimate. They cannot establish equivalence, adequate precision or a general interaction. Formal inference, multiplicity, stopping and meaningful-effect thresholds must be registered before confirmatory outcomes, rather than selected after a favorable pilot.

## Timing and exposure

Early/late is defined relative to B's first **scheduled** decision, even when that decision waits, refuses or is invalid. Defining late treatment after a successful realized relay would condition assignment/delivery on post-treatment behavior. The proposed fixed boundaries avoid that problem. Keep every behaviorally unsuccessful team in its assigned cell; infrastructure failure retains the assignment grid and partial traces while suppressing estimation.

B receives the same final packet multiset across timing levels, but not the same information at its first decision or the same chronological context. Identical final packets therefore do not remove the treatment. C's exposure may differ when B chooses different forwarding or drafts. Do not select only teams with realized corrective receipt, control for their post-treatment inventory, or promote a receipt-conditioned difference into a causal mediator estimate.

The A→C dispatch slot exists in both bypass levels. The factor changes its informative content, rather than removing a network edge. Name mentions and realized references can still differ. Matched source attribution, field layout, token budget and dispatch contacts help define the assigned package; none makes the sham semantically inert or model consumption identical. Record actual recipient-request construction and canonical packet IDs at both C boundaries. These are observable input receipts, not evidence of reading, belief updating or provider consumption.

This probe manipulates correction availability and delivery policy. It does not yet estimate the efficacy of a private evidence-check reminder, an adaptive thought-planting policy or hidden-thought editing. A later reminder contrast could use the same world, but adding a third factor now is unnecessary for the present question.

## Required executable contract

A new adapter must retain immutable source records with distinct revisions, legal bounded forwarding/outboxes, identical action-independent dispatch slots, C-private information and explicit provisional/final answer states. The existing complementary world has four subjects and first-valid-submission semantics; it cannot silently become this three-role revision task. The standalone timing controller also cannot attest a new world's request schema without a scoped adapter. Preserve historical worlds, protocols and results.

Freeze final-answer semantics precisely: a valid `retain` action may commit an existing valid draft; a missing or invalid final response must follow the declared zero rule rather than silently inheriting success from the first draft. Freeze artifact/revision fields, modular value bounds, attachment capacity, free-text budget and rendering order. Authenticate forwarding from B's actual packet inventory; forged or unavailable attachments must fail without inventing a source observation.

The generator makes the latest residue independent and uniform modulo 97. Given only the old residue and C's own residue, the target remains uniform; a correct guess is possible. The stated `1/97` chance assumes scoring the required revision label and modular answer without also requiring a recipient-unknown source hash or a measured exposure gate. Such an additional condition would change both the chance argument and outcome definition. Correctness remains executed reporting, not proof of mental update use.

Before registration, CPU witnesses should establish correct final answers in all four cells—including late/no-informative-bypass—and intentional failures, invalid actions and empty outboxes. Replay must reproduce source slots, scoped requests, retained/revised answers and oracle outcomes. These checks establish feasibility and contract integrity only. The nominal eight-team pilot uses 32 subject decisions; future provider calls require a separately authorized, bounded execution budget. No model effect direction or historical transport follows from this review.
