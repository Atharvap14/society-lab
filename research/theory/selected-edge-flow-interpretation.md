## What the differences describe

Every supported unordered pair is oriented by lexical node ID. Its signal is the forward extracted-reference count minus the reverse count. Both counts and every contributing event ID/hash remain inspectable. Balanced reciprocal events cancel in the coordinate, while their supported edge remains part of the fitting problem. Each April 18 18:00 variant retains one such zero-valued supported edge. Unsupported pairs are absent; no edges are imputed.

The unweighted inner product gives every supported edge one coordinate. It does not weight a pair by traffic volume or observation reliability. Gradient and circulation are fitted signed quantities and can be noninteger even though input counts are integers. A circulation fraction is a proportion of **quadratic net-count energy**, not of messages, agents, successful relays, or information.

For example, in April 17 19:00, exact-baseline circulation energy is about 5.333333 out of signal energy 6. Adding explicit short-name references raises circulation energy to 21.5 while raising signal energy to 69; the fraction therefore falls from about .888889 to .311594. This is not evidence that circulation declined. In April 18 18:00, only the combined Unicode/short-name instrument has a cyclic support; the other three supports are forests. The changed fraction involves a changed measurement/support, not an experimentally changed agent action. Unicode and short-name candidates retain their parent measurement qualifications; this operator does not adjudicate address versus quotation or task-owner reference.

The aggregation **discards time order**. A non-gradient static edge signal establishes no feasible chronological relay, acknowledgment, reading, source use, or causal influence. The [temporal-path interpretation](temporal-path-interpretation.md) remains a separate check. `faces=None` explicitly leaves the two-cell model unspecified: **curl and harmonic components are unavailable**, not zero. No triangle was automatically filled. Potentials use a zero-mean gauge within each component and imply no hierarchy, competence, or agent role.
