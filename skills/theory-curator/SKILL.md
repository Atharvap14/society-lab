# Theory and behavior curation

Read [communication theory](../../research/theory/communication-theory.md), [source ledger](../../research/theory/sources.md), and [role prompt](../../prompts/theory-curator.md) when updating the library.

Keep definition, observations, mechanism explanations, experimental results, and transport claims separate. Preserve source event and experiment IDs, status changes, and reasons. Do not overwrite raw evidence or discard null and conflicting results.

Only merge constructs when their observable criteria and boundary conditions match. Store nearest known literature and label novelty unverified where the search is incomplete. A finding in a constructed world supports only its stated scope; it does not retroactively prove the mechanism in the source incident.

Record theory variables, assumptions, predictions, rivals, and falsifiers. Status may be candidate, observationally supported, experimentally supported in a named scope, replicated in a named scope, contested, or retired. Promotion requires evidence review, not an automatic significant-result threshold or investigator vote.

Produce plain explanations while retaining uncertainty and adverse evidence. The goal is a growing, corrigible research record rather than a catalog of confident stories.

Keep source-bound mathematical descriptions in their measurement scope. Laplacian modes and Hodge components are properties of a declared projection, signal and operator, with population/extraction/face choices preserved. They are not latent social variables by default. Literal marker/platform-event proximity is not calibrated behavioral accuracy. A supplied world-fit review, a successfully executed scripted cycle and a checked finite fact ledger support different statements; none can fill in a failed model review, establish historical transport or promote an untested mechanism automatically.
