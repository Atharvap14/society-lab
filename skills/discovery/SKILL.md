# Behavioral discovery

Use for open-ended hypothesis generation from swarm evidence. Read [communication theory](../../research/theory/communication-theory.md) when defining a new construct, [graph analysis](../../research/theory/graph-analysis.md) for topology or spectral leads, and [discovery prompt](../../prompts/discovery.md) for the output contract.

Start from the assigned evidence packet and tool budget. Validate the raw events behind an anomaly before interpreting its graph. Retrieve exact IDs and nearby boundaries; avoid scanning the whole corpus when a bounded comparison can answer the question.

When evaluating an identifier, read [measurement adjudication](../../research/theory/measurement-adjudication.md). Bind the operational question, retained sampling frame and comparison subset before interpreting agreement. A message review does not label a whole episode or validate the detector's proposed behavior; maintain its declared reviewer provenance and unknown cases.

For mention-network leads, distinguish exact display-name matches, explicit short-name candidates, and Unicode shadow candidates. A quoted model name or historical reference can match without addressing a participant. Review [selected-lead sensitivity](../../research/theory/selected-lead-sensitivity-plan.md): preserve the original candidate/comparator orientation and all source rows, including human rows that affect lexical adjacency. Whole-week metric changes do not establish that a selected episode changes. Native and fixed-node metrics answer different measurement questions; neither supplies a causal effect. Do not silently rematch or promote an expanded extraction instrument into the original discovery.

The [source interpretation example](../../research/theory/selected-lead-interpretation.md) shows why a shrinking or reversed graph contrast does not automatically falsify a narrative candidate. Keep the formal matched windows separate from the investigator's broader source comparisons. A reported update that predates a later request cannot be attributed as its response, and omitted human graph edges do not remove human prompts as common context.

Describe the pattern in observable terms before naming a mechanism. Record event IDs, who could see each event, relevant task constraints, missing context, alternatives, and a counterexample search. Similar wording, centrality, and temporal precedence do not establish influence. A model or harness update, unavailable tool, or logging gap may explain an apparent social event.

Use different investigators for independent initial explanations when that is part of the authorized workflow. Preserve disagreement. Sample ordinary episodes as well as anomalies; anomaly enrichment changes prevalence estimates.

Output a candidate with an operational definition, scope, evidence, rival mechanisms, nearest known literature, falsifier, and smallest discriminating experiment. Return no candidate if the evidence does not support one. Use the schema's `not_applicable` experiment fit for a declined lead; a possible future task substrate does not establish a supported mechanism. An explicit owner, deadline, and normal tool duration can explain a waiting motif without a coordination failure. Separate performed counterexample searches from recommended ones; packet-only tools cannot support a claim to have searched the corpus. Mark novelty unverified until independently checked. Distinguish predefined detectors from preregistered confirmatory experiments.

For time-respecting paths, read [the temporal audit interpretation](../../research/theory/temporal-path-interpretation.md). Preserve the fixed per-window universe and original comparison, examine exact witness timestamps, and distinguish a backward-time representation path from an observed interaction. Do not treat a name-reference direction as identified information flow or use path-order metrics to adjudicate an incoming-concentration hypothesis. Bounded excerpts may omit witnesses; retrieve cited messages before interpreting their contents.

For additional source channels, read [the source-lineage plan and implementation](../../research/theory/source-triangulation-plan.md) and [multiplex experiment framework](../../research/theory/multiplex-communication-experiments.md). Use explicit exported keys with exact table/row/hash bindings; missing or duplicate endpoints stay unknown or ambiguous. Keep emission, reference, exposure, attempted action, artifact transition and task outcome separate. Retrospective prefix examples at other dates cannot validate an assigned graph episode. Do not merge these links into the original mention graph or promote a candidate from a lineage match. Read [the graph-operator research agenda](../../research/theory/graph-operator-research-agenda.md) before interpreting Laplacian rate signals or adding directed/Hodge probes.

Read [actor-window interpretation](../../research/theory/actor-window-interpretation.md) before treating a room-query zero as absence. The actor/time predicate recovers room-missing platform records without assigning them to rooms. Read [literal marker interpretation](../../research/theory/wait-marker-interpretation.md) before treating a waiting phrase as an action label. Inspect exact source text and both marker and nonmarker candidates, including negation, quotation, clock uncertainty, event reuse and censored boundaries. Recorded-coordinate proximity does not establish inactivity or intention-to-action correspondence.

For an edge decomposition, read [Hodge methods](../../research/theory/graph-hodge-methods.md) and [the fixed source plan](../../research/theory/selected-edge-flow-plan.md). Preserve oriented units, original windows, all extraction variants and unavailable cells. Net reference counts can cancel balanced activity; potential and circulation describe that chosen signal only. Undeclared faces leave curl/harmonic unknown. A large circulation fraction is a mathematical lead to inspect, not evidence of rumor, disagreement, hierarchy or a network effect. Test the proposed contact/exposure semantics and chronological feasibility before proposing a social mechanism.
